COMPARATIVE STRUCTURAL PERFORMANCE EVALUATION OF
SHEAR WALL, CORE, OUTRIGGER, DIAGRID, AND HYBRID SYSTEM
IN TALL BUILDING BY USING ETABS


This project focuses on the comparative structural performance evaluation of different lateral load-resisting systems used in tall buildings. The study compares five major structural systems: shear wall system, core system, outrigger system, diagrid system, and hybrid structural system. The main purpose of this research is to identify the most efficient and suitable structural system for tall buildings by evaluating their behavior under gravity loads and lateral loads such as wind and earthquake forces.
Due to rapid urbanization, population growth, and limited availability of land in modern cities, tall buildings have become an important solution for residential, commercial, and office use. However, as building height increases, structural challenges also increase. Tall buildings are highly affected by lateral forces, which may cause excessive displacement
