1350sqft residential home project

This project focuses on Quantity Surveying and Estimation for a residential building located in Jamshoro with a proposed area of 30 ft × 45 ft, equal to 1350 sq. ft. The project includes the estimation of quantities for a ground-floor residential structure consisting of bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, drawing room, car porch, lounge, and other essential spaces. It covers drawings, structural details, earthwork, RCC columns, plinth beams, roof beams, roof slab, DPC, brickwork, plastering, flooring, rate analysis, bill of quantity, preliminary estimate, total project cost, and rate per square foot. The purpose of this project is to understand how civil engineering drawings are converted into measurable quantities and how construction cost is calculated through systematic estimation and rate analysis.
