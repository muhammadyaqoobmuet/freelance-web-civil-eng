End user satisfaction Assessment of ( BRI )



This project examines end-user satisfaction regarding BRI-related projects, especially the Bin Qasim Power Plant. It evaluates how local communities, businesses, government officials, and civil society groups perceive the effects of BRI projects on infrastructure, employment, business growth, environment, transparency, and social integration. The assessment uses mixed-method research, including surveys and interviews, with respondents from countries such as Pakistan, Kenya, Sri Lanka, Ethiopia, Kazakhstan, and Serbia. The project shows that users are generally satisfied with physical infrastructure improvements, but concerns remain regarding environmental protection, transparency, local hiring, and community involvement.
